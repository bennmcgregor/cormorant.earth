# dwho's tiny zine: Trust Yourself

Some guy on the L gave me this poem (he was handing them out to people).

![[poem.jpeg|eager]]

It reads:

*Don't be so eager to do as you're told by powers that be of today  
Doing the bidding of minions and orcs won't keep their evil at bay  
We've known better ways since earliest days  
The wheel is not reinvented  
Remember the lessons learned from lives past  
Old wisdom can be implemented  
If you divine to take up your time with fighting and vengeance for all  
You won't be alone as you slip through your phone into the infernal spiral  
So which way to turn?  
Where can we learn how to proceed in the dark?  
The answer is inside  
It's the thing you can't hide  
The glow that appears from your heart  
It burns like a sun  
You will know it from the way that they must turn away  
Your own light makes clear the right way to steer when the vessel has gone astray  
So when darkness surrounds and demons and hounds of hell are more near than far  
[[note|Trust in yourself]]  
For nothing can kill the being of light that you are*
