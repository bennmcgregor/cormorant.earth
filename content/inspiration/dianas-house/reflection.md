# Diana's House

![[dianas-living-room.mp4]]

The inspiration for this website.

![[columns.mp4]]

It’s a reminder to me that things don’t all have to be useful or functional. You can have things that you just like to look at. 

![[weird-objects-upstairs.jpeg]]
![[upstairs-workspace.jpeg]]
![[glasses-3.jpeg]]
![[glasses-1.jpeg]]

The house is such a strong reflection of who she is, in the stuff she has. It’s very unique. And everyone loves it! It’s a reminder and inspiration to me to stay rooted in my vision and what feels important to me. 

This website is to keep me grounded. A reminder that I’m doing this for myself. 

![[bjork-thought.png]]
![[chemistry-glassware-mantle.jpeg]]
![[antlers.jpeg]]
![[leather-hangings.jpeg]]

The remarkable shelf display, or the weird chemistry glassware, or the display of books and paper on top of, next to, around Roman columns, didn’t just get planned one day. They’re the result of collecting things you find interesting over many years, and then intentionally arranging them in interesting ways. A reminder that interesting and beautiful work emerges out of a consistent practice: it can’t be planned in advance. 

Diana didn’t worry about displaying things perfectly, or displaying perfect things. Many things are pinned to the wall, arranged in antique display cases, half-broken or half-complete. But that’s part of the beauty. 

And that’s the ethos of this website too. It’s my field notes, my world, sketches, ideas, a map, my complete work, incomplete work, my process. It’s a space with beautiful things in it, each with their own story.
