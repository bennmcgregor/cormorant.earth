# Rebecca Solnit on Burtynsky's work

“These places look inhuman, for their scale and for their poisons and hazards, but they're the landscapes on which most human beings now depend.  

It may be that industrial civilization is predicated on blindness and alienation, on not knowing that sweatshops or copper mines make your pleasant First World/suburban existence possible, for that knowledge would at the least make that existence less pleasant."

-Rebecca Solnit, ["Creative Destruction,"](https://www.thenation.com/article/archive/creative-destruction/) The Nation, September 1, 2003
