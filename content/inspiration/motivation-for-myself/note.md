# Motivation for myself

I wrote this note to myself in the middle of the night a couple years ago (2024) when I couldn't sleep thinking about the US election. It still feels true today. It reads:  

***Create the future.** Bring it into reality. Heal. The world is deeply broken. So many people are suffering—including the oppressors.*

*You can't fix everything. You can't heal everyone. But you can heal yourself. You can share your healing with others. You can create a community that sustains you, helps you heal, and gives you the strength to carry on.*

*You can't repair a fundamentally broken system by working within its constraints.*

*You need to create the system you want to see. You need to **create the future.***

*The new is strange. The new is scary. The new is weird. The new is totally, completely, unexpected. It's unrecognizable. You'll probably reject it the first time you see it. So look for these signs to discover the new.*

*Follow your heart, follow your interests. When you blaze a **new** path, this is all you have to lead the way. Relying on anything else will bring you back onto the beaten trail.* 

*Believe in God. Everything is in His hands. You'll be alright. Keep creating. That's all that matters. ["I love thy creation, therefore I created thee."](https://www.bahaiprayers.app/prayer?id=401040).*
