# I dream about clothes.

They come to me: images, feelings, in a fog. I love to analyze and understand the "why," but I never really understand why this idea or another. I just surrender myself to the vision and attempt to recreate it in fabric. Often, it doesn’t work out how I’d envisioned. Sometimes, it takes actually making the clothes the wrong way to understand better what the vision really called for. But more often, the result is this nagging feeling that the piece of clothing I made isn’t quite there. The dream crashes into reality.  

Perhaps this time it will be different?  

Miyazaki and my draw to spirited away, only understanding what it’s about a decade later?
