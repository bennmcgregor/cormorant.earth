## Call in September 2022
This was a very difficult time in my life, and I reached out to Elder Bill for advice. I wrote down some of the things he said to me:

Native knowledge is not an answer to a question, it's an invitation to receive  

Life is not about self-fulfillment, it’s about engaging with your duties as a product of Creation  

What you are experiencing is what you are supposed to experience  

Bill’s doctoral paper:
**Cleaving in same-sex love as a transformative duty to society**
- Compared gay culture to native culture
- Bear society in Six Nations vs Bear culture (gays)
- Native people wear leather vs leather culture (it makes you feel more sexual)
- The type of music that gay men dance to is very tribal, carried out in the dark of the night vs longhouse dances done throughout the night

He studied the recovery of the indigenous mind
