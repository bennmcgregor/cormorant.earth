# Freda Huson on going back to your roots

![go back to your roots](https://youtu.be/bCPBg8xhNzk?si=L8bgvjQuLih1a46N&t=593)
_the video plays at the relevant section_

This had a HUGE impact on me and my thinking around Cormorant. It was thrilling to hear this, like a thunderclap. I was so excited. And I think it's because it connected with what I'd already felt inside of me, but hadn't articulated to myself.

I went up to Freda after the panel discussion and she told me a story about a man she knew who'd become disconnected from his culture. He met someone at an event who helped him figure out where his people had come from—some island in the Caribbean, if I remember correctly. And so he traveled there, and camped out on his ancestral homelands. And when he fell asleep, he dreamed of all his culture's old traditions and practices. He learned so much from his dreams that he was able to open a cultural education centre!

Like... what?? That's crazy!!

Come to think about it, the idea of "going back to your roots" is [[class-assignment|part of what I learned from Elder Bill]]. Except he goes further—asking his students to think about "the migration that brought you to this place." At the end of the day, we are always migrating, putting down new roots in new places.
