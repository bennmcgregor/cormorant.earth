# Renée Lertzman's substack "Becoming Guides"

This article is a basic intro to the blog: https://substack.com/home/post/p-202781600. 

I really liked her article ["hoping the heat will wake us up"](https://reneelertzman.substack.com/p/hoping-the-heat-will-wake-us-up), because it offers a fresh perspective on how to tackle the climate crisis. Her basic argument is that the reason people aren't acting on the climate crisis is primarily because they are overwhelmed: there is no safe place to feel the fear, uncertainty, grief over the climate crisis, and as a result, people are paralyzed and unable to act. This correlates more accurately with contemporary understandings of the human mind. And the task for changemakers is to first create the conditions, environments, spaces for people to feel safe in their feelings over this massive challenge. She calls that "becoming a guide."
