# Mission

Make clothes to go back to our roots.

# Vision

Everyone on the planet wears their authentic style.

# Values

We express our authentic selves.
We are not afraid to be different.
Imperfection is beauty.

We honour where we come from.
We are inspiring our friends to live in harmony with all life.
We do the right thing for our future generations.
We are part of the circle of life.

Working with our hands is the path forwards.


## The original form of these ideas

![[draft-mission-vision-values.png]]

The last one is perhaps the most strange. Why "pooploop"? It's inspired by this exhibition I saw in Tokyo: https://www.2121designsight.jp/en/program/pooploop/. The idea for the name is that it "forc\[es\] a confrontation with things we habitually avoid." i.e., waste and excrement, which are the natural ends of everything—and the beginnings of new cycles. Even though the discomfort is the point, I decided to tone it down in the end...

Why seven generations? It's an idea that originated with the Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) Confederacy: https://www.haudenosauneeconfederacy.com/values/. 

> "In their decision making Chiefs consider how present day decisions will impact their descendants. Nations are taught to respect the world in which they live as they are borrowing it from future generations."

There are some interpretations that span both the past and the future: from your great-grandparents to your great-grandchildren. Regardless if it's 7 generations in the future, or this other interpretation, I like how there is an actual number for the span of time to consider. It helps makes it more concrete. However, I refrained from including it in the actual values because I wasn't sure how to give respectful attribution. Perhaps these paragraphs on my website are sufficient? I'm not entirely sure.
