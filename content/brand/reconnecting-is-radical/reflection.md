# Reconnecting is radical

This Humans of New York video was posted recently: 

<iframe src="https://www.facebook.com/plugins/video.php?height=476&href=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.facebook.com%2Freel%2F2075477139979802%2F&show_text=false&width=267&t=0" width="267" height="476" style="border:none;overflow:hidden" scrolling="no" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="true" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen="true"></iframe>

Humans of New York is radical, because it questions our assumptions about whose stories are worth telling. For example, if we all stopped reporting on Tr\*mp it would sap him of a lot of his power, yet we deem stories about him to be important to tell. So we hand that power over to him. 

Brandon Stanton has been telling a lot of homeless people’s stories recently. By doing it, on such a massive platform, he’s saying that those stories are important.

And what the homeless guy is saying in the video is pretty radical too. It opens your eyes to the source of many things we take for granted, and how much of these basic things are dependent on chance. 

Reconnecting is radical because it’s about seeking the source of things: the roots. [[freda-huson|Going back to your roots.]] (And obviously the [root word of radical is the Latin word for root](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/radical).)

When you go to the source of things you see the true injustice, how so much of what we have is based on to whom we are born, what random things happen to us, our parents, and in our environment throughout our lives. 

Makes me think about Margaret...
