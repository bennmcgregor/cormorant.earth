# Stouffer TP 4x5 31-step wedge

![[wedge-in-action.jpg|eager]]

Found at https://www.stouffer.net/Photo.htm. I realized that this is actually a wedge for projection. The correct contact wedge would be the [T3110](https://www.stouffer.net/TransPage.htm). However, both have the same scale so the calculation still applies. 

Density equivalent: .10  
F-stop equivalent: 1/3  
Dmax: 3.05
