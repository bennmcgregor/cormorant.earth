# Outfit for Ben
*February—July 2025*  
Spring jacket and pants set featuring [Ben's film photography](https://benjaminartharrison.com/).  
Made from midweight [[cotton-canvas|unbleached cotton canvas]], [[cotton-thread|unbleached cotton thread]], [[cyanotype-ingredients|cyanotype dye]], [[materials/aikuma-senryo/kakishibu-dye|kakishibu-dyed]] sashiko thread, [[mother-of-pearl-buttons|mother of pearl buttons]].

![the hardest way to share photos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KVKtl_Ng7M)

This project was the first time I made clothes for someone else. And it shows. Both the jacket and pants went through numerous iterations, where I added extra ease in the side seam to both the jacket and pants, underarm gussets, a pants yoke (to fit the waist better), and waist darts. In the end, I learned a lot about how to streamline the fitting process. 

Given the assignment of printing film photos of detailed subjects, rather than the more abstract patterns of my earlier experiments, I made changes to [[dye-journal#June 2025 improvements|my cyanotype process]] that vastly improved the print quality. 

In June, as the jacket was nearing completion, I began experimenting with kakishibu. Some of my early tests, where I dyed sashiko thread and small pieces of canvas, are on this piece as contrast stitching and other details, such as the hook loop. 

The result was a beautiful, [lyrical](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/bFrTcJVpX4s), and organic matching set forged by the unpredictable process.
