# Aikuma Senryo's washi-linen-cotton fabric

![[washi-linen-cotton-fabric.jpg]]

I bought this on a whim like the [[cormorant.earth/materials/aikuma-senryo/sukumo-dye|sukumo dye]]. I was wondering: how can fabric be made out of paper? It's an extremely sheer fabric that seems to be suited to making [noren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noren). Seems the warp is the cotton/linen blend and thicker and widely spaced, while the washi (sliced into thin strands) is the weft. 

Page I bought from: https://aikuma-japan.com/products/washi-cotton-hemp-mixed-cloth. 

I could not find much information on this, even with the help of Gemini searching in Japanese. The Japanese version of the Aikuma Senryo website didn't even list it.
