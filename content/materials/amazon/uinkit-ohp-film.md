# Uinkit overhead projector film for inkjet

This is the overhead projector film I use for my [[techniques/cyanotype/technique|cyanotype]] negatives: 
https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09WRJ2HNF?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_4&th=1. 

To find the printing side, orient the paper so the rounded corner is in the bottom right. The side you are looking at is the side to be printed on.

[[epson-et-3830|My printer]] cannot print directly on the transparency. It needs an opaque backing in order to print properly. I usually tape a piece of paper to the film.
