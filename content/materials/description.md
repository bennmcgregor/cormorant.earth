# Why record the materials I use?

Cormorant is about [[index#Where am I?|reconnecting]]. Before I started making my own clothes, I had basically no idea how they were made or where they came from.

Recording the materials I use to make my garments is part of understanding where these clothes come from. They come from my hands manipulating these materials to create something new. 

Sourcing my materials is an ongoing process. No one is perfect. Limitations on time and price constrain many of my decisions.

# What do I record?

The ideal would be to record as much information as possible. However, I don't have that kind of time.

So instead I try to record everything I know about the materials when I got them. A link to the page where they're from, what's on the labels, the store, other context. That way, in the future, I have a solid starting point for tracing the origins of the actual materials themselves.
