# Cyanotype Toning


![[scarf-and-pants.jpeg|eager]]

You can shift the colour of a cyanotype print using toning. The iron in the cyanotype pigment functions as a mordant for other pigments to bind to the fabric, so it is actually a fairly durable dye technique. 

I learned most of what I know about cyanotype toning from [Annette Golaz's book on the subject](https://www.agolaz.ch/about-1/publications/).
