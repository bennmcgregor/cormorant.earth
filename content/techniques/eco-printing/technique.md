# Eco-printing

![[lining-midway.jpg|eager]]
_The lining from [[cormorant.earth/outfits/cathy/piece|Cathy's outfit]] mid-way through eco-printing_

Eco-printing is the technique of printing fabric with plant material. It's a form of [[cormorant.earth/techniques/natural-dye/technique|natural dyeing]], but is a [relatively new phenomenon](https://www.domestika.org/en/blog/5013-a-brief-history-of-botanical-printing). It involves bundling mordanted fabric with plant material and steaming.

I learned a lot from the book ["The Fundamentals of Eco-Printing"](https://www.annacarolynmeier.com/product-page/the-fundamentals-of-eco-printing-3) by Anna Carolyn Meier.
