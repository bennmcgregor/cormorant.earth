# Thoughts on French seams

For wovens, all you ever need are straight stitches. And that's because of French seams! They enclose the fraying edges of the fabric. Overlocker or zigzag stitches unnecessary. (You can also use flat fell seams for greater strength).

I was first introduced to this concept by my friend Kennedy, who went to sewing school. He told me that he personally hates the look of overlocking stitches, and prefers to use French seams for everything. After that I started to switch over too (from zigzag stitches). French seams are definitely "fancier" looking (and an increasingly rare mark of high quality in stores). Although they do make changes more difficult after the fact. Now, I try to sew the garment together inside out and test fit before I complete the French seams.
